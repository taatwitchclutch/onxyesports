🎓 ONYX ACADEMY (Development Team)

Welcome to the ONYX Academy, where aspiring developers can enhance their skills and grow with the ONX team.

Purpose

The purpose of the Academy is to provide a structured learning environment for those interested in game development and support our community.

Requirements

  • Passion for game development.
  • Basic knowledge of programming languages.
  • A commitment to the Academy's schedule and learning modules.

Benefits

  • Hands-on experience with real projects.
  • Mentorship from experienced developers.
  • Opportunity to join the ONX Development Team after completion.

Promotion System

Academy members can be promoted based on their performance, project contributions, and collaboration with peers.

Rules

  • Respect others' opinions and contributions.
  • Complete assignments on time.
  • Participate actively in discussions and projects.
